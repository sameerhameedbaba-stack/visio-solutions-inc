Solution

Modernize an existing application

Improve aging systems without a risky big-bang rewrite.

Legacy systems can be costly to maintain and hard to change, yet too central to replace overnight. This solution modernizes incrementally — carving out and improving parts in a sequence that reduces risk while keeping operations stable.

Is this you?

Signals that this is your situation

If several of these ring true, this solution is likely relevant.

  • A legacy system is costly to maintain and hard to change
  • The system is too central to replace all at once
  • Knowledge of the system is concentrated in a few people
  • Change carries real operational risk
Approach

How an engagement typically unfolds

  1. 1

    Assess and plan

    Understand the system and sequence the work.

  2. 2

    Carve out and replace

    Modernize parts incrementally behind clear boundaries.

  3. 3

    Integrate and migrate

    Keep systems working together through the transition.

  4. 4

    Stabilize

    Tests, documentation, and reduced key-person risk.
